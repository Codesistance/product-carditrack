# ARPA-H commits $62.7M to build FDA-authorized agentic AI for heart failure care

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** competition
**Date found:** 2026-09-12 (announced 2026-09-09)

## Summary

ARPA-H announced its four-year, $62.7M "ADVOCATE" program on 2026-09-09,
funding six teams — including Kaiser Permanente, Duke, Stanford, and AI
vendors Tempus AI, Atman Health, and Updoc — to build FDA-authorized
autonomous AI agents delivering 24/7 cardiovascular care between clinician
visits. Target: FDA authorization within ~3 years.

This is direct, well-funded federal validation of the "agentic proactive
care" thesis on CardiTrack's roadmap — backed by top-tier health systems and
real AI vendors rather than a single startup. It's provider/patient-facing
clinical infrastructure, not family-caregiver-facing, and years from
shipping, but the participating AI vendors (Tempus AI especially) are
credible future competitors or partners once authorized.

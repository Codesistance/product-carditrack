# Correction: EIC Accelerator's next cutoff is 4 November 2026, not 6 October 2026

**Severity:** FYI (correction to a previously logged item)
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The log's 2026-09-04 entry for EIC Accelerator (Open) listed "next cutoff 2026-10-06."
The confirmed 2026 Work Programme cutoff calendar is bimonthly: 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May,
8 Jul, **2 Sep**, **4 Nov** — there is no October date anywhere in the schedule. The 2
September cutoff has already passed as of this run; the actual next full-proposal
cutoff is **4 November 2026**. Step 1 (short proposal) remains submittable on a rolling
basis via the Funding & Tenders Portal regardless of the Step 2 cutoff. Grant-only track
for UK (non-associated country) applicants remains up to ~€2.5m, unchanged.

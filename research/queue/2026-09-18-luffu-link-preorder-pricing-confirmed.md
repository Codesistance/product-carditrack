**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** competition

## Summary

Preorders opened 2026-09-16 at $249.99 (intro price, $50 off the $299.99 planned retail), with US shipments confirmed for early 2027. Requires a separate Luffu Family Plan ($19.99/mo or $199.99/yr) for up to four members. The screenless LTE band records background HR/HRV/breathing/sleep plus voice-logged notes on meds, symptoms and mood, surfaced through a family-caregiving app now in public beta; a companion in-home display is planned later in 2026.

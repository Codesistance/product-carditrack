# Heart Research UK Novel & Emerging Technologies Grant — up to £300k, outline deadline 2 Oct 2026

## Summary
Heart Research UK (UK cardiovascular research charity) runs an annual Novel & Emerging Technologies (NET) grant funding technology-focused research that clearly benefits heart disease patients. The 2026 round accepts outline applications from **2 September 2026 to 2 October 2026**, via the FlexiGrant grants management system. Awards are for up to **£300,000** (no indirect costs), and the research must be focused solely on heart disease with a clearly articulated patient benefit.

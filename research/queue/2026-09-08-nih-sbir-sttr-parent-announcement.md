# NIH SBIR/STTR parent announcements — next standard due date 2027-01-05 (US, gated on a US entity)

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** grants

## Summary

NIH's standing SBIR (PA-27-100) / STTR (PA-27-102) omnibus parent
announcements have no fixed topic; next standard receipt date is
**2027-01-05**. Phase I is typically ≤$314k (~1 year), Phase II ≤$2.3M (~2
years). Digital health/AI and point-of-care diagnostics are named HHS
priority areas.

## Sources

- https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding/sbir-sttr-funding-opportunities (NIH SEED — primary)

## Why it matters to CardiTrack

Flagged per the US-second jurisdiction priority since UK/EU options are thin
this cycle, but there is a hard eligibility barrier: NIH SBIR/STTR requires
the applicant be a "small business concern" that is majority US-owned/
controlled and primarily performs the work in the US. CardiTrack, as a UK
entity, would need a properly structured US subsidiary to qualify — a real
structural/business decision, not a paperwork step. Deadline is nearly 4
months out, well outside the grants HIGH bar (30 days), and gated on that
unresolved structural question, so FYI rather than HIGH.

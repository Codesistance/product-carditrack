# NIHR i4i Product Development Awards — October 2026 call opens 2026-10-07, outline applications due 1pm 2026-12-02

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The NIHR Invention for Innovation (i4i) Product Development Award (PDA) is the main UK public
funder for taking a medical device, IVD or "high-impact, patient-focused digital health
technology" towards NHS or social-care adoption. The next round is expected to launch
**2026-10-07**, with **outline applications due 1pm on 2026-12-02** and full applications by
invitation between 2027-02-11 and 2027-04-01. Scope and eligibility are expected to follow the
April 2026 round: collaborative, two-stage, an SME may lead but needs a co-applicant from another
organisation (typically an NHS Trust or university). Full guidance is not yet published.

**Caveat on the dates:** the NIHR programme page is proxy-blocked from the digest sandbox; the
dates come from a grant-consultancy notice of 2026-09-09 citing NIHR. Confirm on nihr.ac.uk when
the call goes live.

## Why flagged

Grants, UK first. This is the best-fitting open-window UK call found this week, and its outline
deadline is ten weeks away — enough runway to line up an NHS or academic co-applicant, which is
exactly the evidence gap the NIA 2027 brief (2026-09-09) identified. One caution: PDA funds
products with a *medical purpose*. Framing CardiTrack's caregiver alerts as a clinical evaluation
pulls it toward the SaMD side of the line the regulation briefs track; that is a positioning
decision, not a paperwork one.

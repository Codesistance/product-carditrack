# Unannounced Fitbit-family hardware spotted in FCC filing (Goertek-built, Google-branded)

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** devices
**Date flagged:** 2026-09-17

## Summary

A device (model G8BL6, FCC ID SZGG8BL6) was filed with the FCC on
2026-09-10, built by Goertek — the same manufacturer that built Fitbit Air
and several generations of Pixel Buds. The filing confirms Bluetooth LE and
GNSS, no Wi-Fi (phone-paired), and a regulatory-label path (Settings >
System > Regulatory Information) matching the Pixel Watch's software
convention rather than classic Fitbit firmware — pointing toward Wear OS
rather than Fitbit's own OS. A follow-up Singapore IMDA listing on
2026-09-14 confirmed Google, not a third party, as the device's owner. No
vendor press release yet; likely the rumoured "Fitbit Charge 7" or a new
Pixel-branded tracker, expected to be officially announced later in 2026.

## Why flagged

Roadmap intelligence on a vendor CardiTrack actually integrates with
(Fitbit is one of only two connectable device types). No action needed

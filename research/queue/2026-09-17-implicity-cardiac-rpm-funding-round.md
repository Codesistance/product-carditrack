# Implicity raises €35–40M to scale AI cardiac remote monitoring + heart-failure prediction into the US

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** competition
**Date flagged:** 2026-09-17

## Summary

French startup Implicity (founded 2016) closed a €35M growth-equity round
(IRIS, Five Arrows/Rothschild) on 2026-09-09 to fund US expansion and
next-generation heart-failure prediction algorithms. It already monitors
120,000+ patients per day across 250+ centres in the US, France, and
Germany, aggregating data from cardiac implantable devices (not consumer
wearables — this is the key open question, see below). Implicity publishes
its own outcomes data: a claimed 26% mortality reduction and 4% fewer
hospitalisations.

This is the closest-matching, best-funded, most clinically-validated direct
competitor found to date against CardiTrack's core thesis (AI-assisted
cardiac remote monitoring with predictive risk routing), and it now has
capital specifically earmarked for US market entry and HF-prediction R&D —
both areas CardiTrack is also building toward.

## Why flagged

A funded competitor scaling a directly overlapping product into a new
market — meets the HIGH bar ("a competitor ships something on our
roadmap").

# AutoMapper 16.2.0 confirmed under a commercial dual-license — free Community tier caps at $5M annual revenue

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** dependencies
**Source:** https://www.jimmybogard.com/automapper-and-mediatr-going-commercial/

## Summary

AutoMapper (and MediatR) moved to a dual license under Jimmy Bogard's Lucky Penny Software; v16.2.0 ships under that license. Free Community use covers individuals and companies with under $5M USD annual revenue (non-profits: under $5M annual budget); above that, a per-team-size commercial license is required.

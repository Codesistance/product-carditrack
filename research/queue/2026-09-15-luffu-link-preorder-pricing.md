# Luffu Link opens preorders: $249.99, $19.99-29.99/mo family plans, early-2027 shipping

**Category:** competition · **Severity:** HIGH · **Status:** update to the 2026-09-09 item (same URL)

## Summary

Since the 2026-09-09 flag (Fitbit founders' family-caregiving wearable),
Luffu has published concrete commercial terms: **$249.99 preorder** (vs.
$299.99 planned retail), a **family subscription at $19.99/mo or $199.99/yr**
covering up to 4 members (an extended plan covers up to 8 at $29.99/mo), and
**US shipping in early 2027**. The device is screenless/single-button with
voice-logged symptoms/meds/mood alongside passive HR/HRV/breathing-rate/sleep
tracking; a companion in-home display is planned later in 2026.

## Sources

- https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link
- https://luffu.com/pages/order

## Why flagged

This sharpens the competitive threat from "directly competitive positioning"
to a concrete family-subscription price point and feature set — voice-logged
symptoms/meds alongside passive vitals — that overlaps CardiTrack's caregiver
dashboard and AI-narrated digest ambitions. The ~4-month runway before
shipping is a real but narrowing window to differentiate on AI-narrated
severity alerts, which Luffu's public materials don't yet describe.

# FDA discussion paper proposes a "competency assessment" framework for generative-AI medical devices — comments open to 2026-10-19

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

FDA's Digital Health Center of Excellence (CDRH) published "Considerations for the
Regulation of Generative AI-Enabled Medical Devices: Discussion Paper and Request for
Feedback" on 2026-08-18. It proposes a "competency assessment" premarket-evaluation concept
— modeled loosely on how clinicians are trained and tested — combining non-clinical
benchmarking with clinical confirmation, plus postmarket-monitoring ideas specific to
generative-AI outputs. It is explicitly a request for feedback, not binding guidance; the
public comment period closes **2026-10-19**.

regulator-specific framework proposal aimed specifically at a generative-AI narration layer
— exactly what MedGemma is in CardiTrack's pipeline — rather than at SaMD software
generally. Non-binding today, but worth tracking as the earliest signal of how any
regulator might eventually evaluate that layer.

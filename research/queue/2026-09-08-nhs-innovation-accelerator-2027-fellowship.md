# NHS Innovation Accelerator — 2027 Fellowship cohort, deadline 2026-09-27

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The NHS Innovation Accelerator (NIA), run by the Health Innovation
Network/NHS England Accelerated Access Collaborative, is open for its 2027
Fellowship cohort. Deadline **2026-09-27, 23:59**. Up to £50k bursary (paid
quarterly against Sprint Plans) plus a 3-year NHS scale-up fellowship —
mentorship and national NHS network access, no equity taken. Open to
innovators with real-world evidence of impact and clear scale-up ambition.
The 2027 cohort explicitly prioritises "access" (predictive prevention,
digital front door, interoperability) and "productivity" (digitally enabled
care, community care, reducing waste).

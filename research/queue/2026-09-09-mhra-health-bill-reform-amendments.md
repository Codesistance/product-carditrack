# MHRA regulatory reform amendments tabled in the Health Bill

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

The UK Government tabled three MHRA-related amendments to the Health Bill on 2026-09-01:
broader MHRA information-sharing powers with trusted government bodies and global regulators;
a mechanism letting device legislation auto-update to reflect technical standards/international
guidelines with lighter-touch consultation for minor changes; and enabling powers for a
possible future medical-device licensing regime, under which GB-market devices placed via a
domestic route could eventually need an MHRA-issued licence rather than relying solely on
third-party conformity assessment (the current UKCA route). MHRA states explicitly there are no
immediate changes to medical device regulatory requirements.

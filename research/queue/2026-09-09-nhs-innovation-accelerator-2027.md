# NHS Innovation Accelerator (NIA) 2027 Cohort — deadline 2026-09-27

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The NHS Innovation Accelerator is now taking applications for its 2027 Fellowship cohort
(applications opened 2026-08-17, close **2026-09-27 23:59** — 18 days from this run). It is not
a traditional R&D cash grant but an NHS-run scale-up fellowship: a bursary of up to £30,000 per
Fellow (funded via AHSNs) plus a 3-year structured support package — an NHS Navigator brokering
procurement/adoption introductions, mentoring, and access to all 15 NHS Health Innovation
Networks. 2027-cohort priority areas include access, productivity and modern service
frameworks, which a cardiac remote-monitoring/AI-triage platform speaks to directly.

The fit condition matters: NIA is aimed at **scaling proven solutions** with real-world
evidence, not early-stage product development. Only worth pursuing once CardiTrack has
NHS-side deployment or outcomes evidence to point to.

# EIC Accelerator: the "2026-10-06" cutoff we logged does not exist — real schedule is six bimonthly dates

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The 2026-09-04 digest logged the EIC Accelerator (Open) next cutoff as **2026-10-06**.
That date does not appear anywhere in the EIC's actual 2026 Work Programme. The real 2026
schedule moved from two calls/year to **six bimonthly full-proposal cut-offs**:
2026-01-07, 2026-03-04, 2026-05-06, 2026-07-08, **2026-09-02**, 2026-11-04.

The 2026-09-02 cutoff has already passed (4 days before this digest run). The next real
opportunity is **2026-11-04**. UK-applicant eligibility for the grant-only track
(up to ~€2.5m) is unchanged.

## Why this matters for CardiTrack

If any internal planning was built around "next cutoff: 2026-10-06," that plan needs
correcting now — that date will never occur, and a submission prepared for it would miss
every real deadline in the schedule. This also means the 2026-09-02 window is gone;
anything not submitted by then has to wait for 2026-11-04.

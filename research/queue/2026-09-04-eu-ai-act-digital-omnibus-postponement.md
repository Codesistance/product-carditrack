# EU AI Act — Digital Omnibus postpones high-risk obligations

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

The Council gave final approval on 2026-06-29 to the "Digital Omnibus" simplification
package. Annex III (stand-alone) high-risk AI obligations move from 2026-08-02 to
2027-12-02; Annex I obligations (AI embedded in already-regulated products, e.g. medical
devices) move from 2027-08-02 to 2028-08-02. AI already regulated under MDR/IVDR gets an
extra year to meet Article 6(1) requirements.

## Why flagged

If MedGemma's severity-verdict pipeline were ever classified as high-risk AI (health-
adjacent triage/alerting is exactly the kind of use Annex III targets), the compliance
runway just got materially longer. This reduces near-term urgency but does not change
the underlying classification question — it is schedule relief, not a verdict.

# Correction: EIC Accelerator has no October full-proposal cutoff

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants
**Date found:** 2026-09-12

## Summary

The 2026-09-04 digest entry for `eic-accelerator-open` stated "next cutoff
2026-10-06." That date is actually a monthly short-proposal (Step 1) batch
date, not a full-proposal cutoff. The 2026 full-proposal cutoffs are 7 Jan,
4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, and **4 Nov** — there is no October full-proposal
cutoff. UK applicants remain eligible on the grant-only track (~€2.5m
ceiling).

Anyone planning around the previously-reported Oct 6 date as a submission
deadline would be planning against the wrong milestone.

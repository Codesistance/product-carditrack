# Implicity raises €35M (~$40M) to scale AI-driven cardiac remote-monitoring platform

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** competition

## Summary

Implicity, whose AI aggregates and normalizes cardiac implantable-device (CIED) and
heart-failure telemetry across manufacturers, raised €35M in growth equity on 2026-09-09 to
expand into the US. The company reports 26% lower mortality for monitored patients across 250+
centers and 120k+ patients, and the round funds US scale-up.

Like the already-logged Cadence AI item (`research/queue/2026-09-04-cadence-ai-proactive-care-engine.md`),
this is a clinician/EMR/medical-center workflow product — no family-caregiver-facing surface —
so it does not compete directly with CardiTrack's positioning. Flagged for awareness of the
broader cardiac-AI-monitoring funding environment, not as a direct roadmap threat.

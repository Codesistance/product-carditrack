# Digital Europe Programme opens €63.2M AI-in-health call bundle, incl. €9M for cardiovascular screening — deadline 2026-10-01

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The European Commission opened seven Digital Europe Programme calls worth €63.2M for
AI innovation in health and online safety, closing via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal
on **2026-10-01** (15 days from this run). Within the bundle: **€9M specifically for
AI-powered image/signal screening in medical centres for early detection of cancer and
cardiovascular disease**, and €24.4M for digital health services under the European
Health Data Space. The cardiovascular-screening sub-call is the closest direct
sector/product fit found across all grant tracking to date for CardiTrack — closer than
the general-purpose EIC/EIT calls already on the log.

# EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duty is already in force — CardiTrack has no AI-disclosure on MedGemma narration

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Article 50 of the EU AI Act — the general-purpose transparency obligation for
AI systems that interact with natural persons — has been in force since
**2026-08-02**. It requires that a person be informed they are interacting
with, or viewing output generated by, an AI system, unless this is obvious
from the context. This is a separate, already-binding obligation, distinct
from (and much narrower than) the high-risk Annex III/Annex I obligations
that the Digital Omnibus postponed to Dec 2027/Aug 2028 (already tracked in
this log) — Article 50 was not part of that postponement and proceeded on
schedule.

Cross-checked against CardiTrack's own codebase and docs (not just the
regulation): the mobile and web UI's "CardiTrack Insights" card — the surface
that displays MedGemma-generated narration to caregivers — carries only a
generic medical disclaimer ("These observations are here to help — but
always talk to a doctor before making health decisions"), per
`docs/execution/ui/mobile/ui_screens_maui_mobile.md:1818` and
`docs/execution/ui/web/ui_screens_blazor_web.md:1507`. Nothing in that copy,
or anywhere else searched in the mobile/web UI, states that the insight text
was generated by an AI model. The only AI/data-related disclosure that exists
in the product is `HealthDataDisclosureBanner` (`User.HealthDataDisclosureDismissedDate`,
migration `20260805110125_AddUserHealthDataDisclosureDismissed`) — and that is
Google's *prescribed health-data-collection* disclosure ("CardiTrack collects
health and fitness data to enable anomaly alerts, daily health digests, and
trend monitoring"), required for the Google Health API restricted-scope
review, not an AI-content disclosure. It is also, per
`docs/technical/user_onboarding_process.md:268`, **shipped on web only —
still missing on mobile**, which is the primary client.

## Why it matters to CardiTrack

This is not a SaMD-boundary question (the already-tracked FDA/MHRA/EU MDR
watch) — it is a separate, already-active EU obligation that plausibly
applies the moment MedGemma-generated text is shown to an EU caregiver. "Not
a medical device" does not mean "not subject to the AI Act." The compliance
date has already passed (2026-08-02), over a month before this run, and
CardiTrack's current UI copy does not appear to satisfy it on either
platform.

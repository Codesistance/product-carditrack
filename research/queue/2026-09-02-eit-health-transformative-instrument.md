# EIT Health — Transformative Healthcare Instrument 2026

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

€300,000–€500,000 grant (covering up to 50% of project costs, co-funding required) for
high-potential European SMEs in digital health/AI/medtech (minimum 4 FTEs, at least €2m equity
raised in the last 36 months) to accelerate product development and prepare for a further funding
round.

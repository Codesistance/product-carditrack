# ARPA-H launches $62.7M "ADVOCATE" program for agentic AI cardiovascular care

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** competition

## Summary

ARPA-H announced a 4-year, $62.7M contract on 2026-09-09 for "Agentic AI-Enabled CardioVascular
CAre TransfOrmation" (ADVOCATE): building an autonomous "digital member of the care team" for
heart-failure patients in the gaps between clinical visits. Named participants: Atman Health,
Updoc, and Tempus AI building the patient-facing agent; Stanford building a supervisory oversight
agent; Duke and Kaiser Permanente running real-world deployment for rural/underserved
populations. The program targets an FDA authorization package within two years.

This lands squarely on CardiTrack's own "proactive/agentic care coordination for cardiac
patients" roadmap direction — the same territory as the already-logged Ejenta item
(`research/queue/2026-09-04-ejenta-heart-failure-agentic-ai.md`) — but now backed by federal
funding and a serious AI vendor (Tempus AI) rather than a single startup, plus an academic
oversight partner (Stanford) explicitly tasked with the safety/supervision problem CardiTrack
also has to solve for MedGemma-narrated alerts.

## Why flagged

Raises the credible-competitor bar in agentic cardiac care the same way Ejenta did in March —
this one is federally funded, multi-institution, and has a stated FDA-clearance timeline (2
years), which is a faster and better-resourced path to a clinically-authorized competitor than
most startups can match alone.

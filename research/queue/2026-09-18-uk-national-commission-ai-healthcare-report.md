# UK National Commission recommends a 'staged authorisation' (L-plate) model for AI-in-healthcare — the clearest SaMD/AIaMD boundary signal yet for MedGemma-style narration features

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation
**Source:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-commission-into-the-regulation-of-ai-in-healthcare-recommendations-for-a-future-regulatory-framework/national-commission-into-the-regulation-of-ai-in-healthcare-recommendations-for-a-future-regulatory-framework

## Summary

The independent National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare (established by MHRA Sept 2025, chaired by Prof. Alastair Denniston) published its final report 2026-09-10 after a year-long consultation (12,000+ respondents). Headline recommendation: a 'staged authorisation' model — new AI models operate under close supervision before being granted fuller authorisation — plus lifecycle-based, system-wide oversight rather than one-off premarket clearance. MHRA's CEO called it 'the most detailed roadmap yet'. A formal MHRA/government response is expected 'in due course'; full implementation (subject to legislation) is not expected before 2027.

## Why flagged

No compliance deadline yet, so it doesn't meet the CRITICAL bar — but a staged/L-plate model could create a new intermediate regulatory tier between 'general wellness' and full SaMD that a narration-only feature like MedGemma's could fall into even without autonomous decisioning. This is the report to watch for the formal follow-up response.

## Next question / action

When MHRA/government issues its formal response, check whether it proposes a concrete staged-authorisation pathway with a compliance date, and whether CardiTrack's narration-only MedGemma feature would fall inside or outside that tier.

# FDA discussion paper: regulating generative-AI-enabled medical devices

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

FDA's Digital Health Center of Excellence published its first substantive framing of how it might
regulate generative-AI/foundation-model-based medical devices: a "competency assessment" model for
premarket evaluation (modeled on how physicians are trained/tested), risk-proportionate postmarket
monitoring, and foundation-model/agentic-AI-specific considerations. Explicitly discussion-only —
not draft or final guidance, no proposed rule yet. Public comment period open, docket
FDA-2026-N-7874, closing **19 October 2026**.

## Why flagged

CardiTrack's core architecture — a generative model (MedGemma) interpreting deterministic outputs
and producing caregiver-facing summaries and severity routing — is exactly the class of system this
paper is scoping. It's the clearest early signal of where US expectations for gen-AI clinical tools
are heading, and the comment window is a live opportunity to help shape that framework before it
hardens into guidance, ahead of any US market plans.

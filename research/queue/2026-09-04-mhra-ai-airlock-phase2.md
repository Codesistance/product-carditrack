# MHRA — AI Airlock Phase 2 complete; dedicated AI-in-medical-devices framework expected later in 2026

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

MHRA's AI Airlock regulatory-sandbox Phase 2 report has been published; Phase 3 is
funded (£1.2m/yr, 2026-2029) and is translating pilot findings into "actionable
regulatory guidance." MHRA has confirmed a dedicated AI-in-medical-devices framework is
coming in 2026, informed directly by Airlock findings.

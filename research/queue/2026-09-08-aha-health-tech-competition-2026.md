# American Heart Association Health Tech Competition 2026 — deadline 2026-09-18

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

AHA's global Health Tech Competition 2026 is open, deadline **2026-09-18**
(finalists announced 2026-10-10; live pitches 2026-11-07/08 at AHA Scientific
Sessions, Chicago; winner announced 2026-11-09). Non-cash prize: 1-year AHA
Innovators' Network membership, executive mentorship, and AHA-channel
promotion for the winner; five finalists get a live-pitch platform at
Scientific Sessions. Open globally to market-ready companies with
clinical/pilot data and a viable business model. Scope explicitly names AI,
wearables, remote monitoring, and predictive analytics for heart failure,
arrhythmias, hypertension, stroke and cardiometabolic disease, with emphasis
on clinical validation over demo-stage pitching.

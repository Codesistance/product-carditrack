# Luffu Link — Fitbit's founders launch an AI family-caregiving wearable

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** competition

## Summary

James Park and Eric Friedman (Fitbit's original founders) launched Luffu Link, a screenless LTE
wearable band, as the hardware companion to their AI family-health platform Luffu (app launched
Feb 2026). Luffu's stated model is close to CardiTrack's own positioning: passive HR/HRV/breathing
tracking, AI that learns family members' patterns in the background, and proactive alerts for
"unusual vitals" or missed medications surfaced to a shared family view — AI-interpreted
vital-signs summaries with caregiver/family alerting, not just raw data.

## Why flagged

This is a well-funded, credible team — Fitbit's own founders — shipping close to CardiTrack's exact
niche (AI-assisted vitals interpretation + severity-style alerting to family/caregivers), with a
live hardware product and public subscription pricing already announced (Link hardware $249.99
preorder, ships early 2027; Family Plan $19.99/mo for 4 members, Extended $29.99/mo for 8, or
$199.99/yr). This is the clearest "competitor moving onto our roadmap" signal in this run.

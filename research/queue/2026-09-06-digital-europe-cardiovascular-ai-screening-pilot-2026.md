# New EU grant call touches cardiovascular AI — but requires a 7-partner, 5-country consortium (deadline 2026-10-01)

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

The Digital Europe Programme opened call **DIGITAL-2026-AI-PILOTING-10-SCREENING**
("Apply AI: Piloting AI-based image screening in medical centres for cancer and
cardiovascular diagnostics"): €9m total (2 projects, up to €4.5m each) for large-scale,
multi-country clinical pilots of cloud-based AI systems for medical image screening,
including cardiovascular imaging, with explicit deliverables on EU AI Act / MDR
compliance evidence for reimbursement pathways. Opened 2026-04-21, closes
**2026-10-01 17:00 Brussels time** — inside the 30-day HIGH-severity window from today.

## Why this matters for CardiTrack — and the catch

The cardiovascular-AI-plus-EU-compliance angle is squarely on topic. But eligibility
requires a consortium of **at least 7 applicants across at least 5 countries**, including
hospital networks and at least one industrial partner. CardiTrack cannot apply as a
standalone or lead applicant. This is only actionable if CardiTrack could join an
existing consortium as an AI/software technology partner — otherwise it's a watch item,
not a submission target.

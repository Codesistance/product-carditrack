# Fitbit Air launched — $99 screenless tracker with AFib alerts

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** devices

## Summary

Google shipped Fitbit Air (2026-05), a $99 screenless band with 24/7 heart rate, HRV,
SpO2, sleep, and irregular-rhythm (AFib) alerts, bundled with a 3-month Google Health
Premium trial, syncing through the Google Health app.

## Sources

- https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/fitbit-air/

## Why flagged

Live-platform news (Fitbit is one of CardiTrack's two connectable providers), but purely
positive and requires no integration work: a cheap, cardiac-capable device on an
already-supported platform lowers the adoption barrier for wearers (who never log in
themselves — someone still has to buy and wear the device). Good for growing the
addressable install base.

# EU AI Act — Digital Omnibus defers high-risk AI compliance deadlines

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 ("Digital Omnibus on AI"), in force 27 July 2026, defers the general
application date for standalone high-risk AI systems (Annex III) from 2 August 2026 to
**2 December 2027**. For AI systems embedded in products already regulated under other EU
product-safety law (Annex I — including AI embedded in or qualifying as a medical device under
MDR/IVDR), the deadline moves from 2 August 2027 to **2 August 2028**. The Commission cited
industry/standards-body (CEN/CENELEC) unreadiness as the reason.

## Why flagged

Real deadline relief, not a boundary change: if CardiTrack's MedGemma-based severity routing were
ever found to meet the medical-device/high-risk-AI threshold, this pushes the compliance clock back
by roughly one to two years. It does not change the underlying question of whether the current
feature set crosses that line — only how much runway exists once it does.

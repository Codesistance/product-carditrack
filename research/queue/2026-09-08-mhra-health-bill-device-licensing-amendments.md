# MHRA tables Health Bill amendments enabling a future GB medical-device licensing regime

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

On 2026-09-02 the UK government tabled three MHRA-related amendments to the
Health Bill: (1) clearer powers to share device/medicines information with
trusted UK and international regulators; (2) enabling legislation to
auto-update regulations in line with future international technical
standards; (3) enabling powers, amending the Medicines and Medical Devices
Act 2021, to found a future GB medical-device *licensing* regime — an
MHRA-issued licence potentially sitting alongside or replacing third-party
UKCA conformity assessment for domestic-route devices. These are enabling
powers only; no immediate rule changes, and further consultation is required
before anything operative exists.

operationalized in Great Britain. Worth knowing about now so the eventual
framework announcement isn't a surprise about *how* licensing would work, not
just *what* it covers.

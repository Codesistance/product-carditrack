# Correction: EIC Accelerator's next full-application cutoff is 2026-11-04, not the 2026-10-06 recorded on 2026-09-04

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** grants
**Date recorded:** 2026-09-26

## Summary

The 2026-09-04 digest recorded the EIC Accelerator (Open) with "next cutoff 2026-10-06". This run's
grants sweep found no such date. A search restricted to eic.ec.europa.eu returns the Work Programme
2026 full-application batching dates as **7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep and 4 Nov 2026**
(bimonthly); consultancy pages (Zabala, Alientt, Catalyze, Innovation-Manager) list the same set.
The next cutoff after today is **2026-11-04**. Short applications remain open continuously, with a
4–6 week evaluation, and a positive short-application result is required before a full application
can be submitted to a batch — so the November batch is reachable only by a company whose short
application is already approved.

The draft EIC Work Programme 2027 ("DRAFT 07.07", unadopted) reduces the Accelerator to four cutoffs:
3 Feb, 1 Apr, 1 Sep and 3 Nov 2027. Held as draft.

The EIC page is proxy-blocked from the sandbox, so neither the original entry nor this correction has
been read at source. The 2026 dates are consistent across every indexed source; the 6 October date
appears in none.

A wrong deadline on the tracker is worse than no deadline: the parent message has repeated 2026-10-06
for three weeks. Publishing the correction against the same URL keeps the dedup record honest and
moves the tracker to 2026-11-04.

# Correction: EIC Accelerator's next cutoff is 4 Nov 2026, not 6 Oct — 2026 Work Programme runs bimonthly with no October date

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** grants
**Source:** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator/eic-accelerator-open_en

## Summary

The '6 Oct 2026' cutoff reported on 2026-09-04 does not match the actual 2026 EIC Work Programme (adopted Nov 2025, amended March and 17 June 2026). Since the overhaul, the Accelerator runs on a bimonthly full-proposal cutoff cycle with no October date: 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, and 4 Nov 2026. The 2 Sep cutoff has already passed; the next real cutoff is 4 Nov 2026 — 47 days out, outside the 30-day HIGH threshold.

## Why flagged

Corrects a previously-reported date that would otherwise cause a missed or mistimed application window.

## Next question / action

Track 4 Nov 2026 as the next real cutoff going forward, not 6 Oct.

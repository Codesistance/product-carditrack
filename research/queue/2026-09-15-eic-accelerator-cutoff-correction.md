**Category:** grants · **Severity:** FYI · **Status:** correction to the 2026-09-04 item (same URL)

## Summary

The previously tracked "next cutoff 2026-10-06" does not match the official
2026 schedule. Confirmed full-proposal cutoffs for 2026 are: 7 Jan, 4 Mar,
6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, and **4 Nov**. The Sept 2 cutoff has already passed; the
next real cutoff is 2026-11-04 (50 days out — not itself inside the 30-day
HIGH threshold, but the date on file was simply wrong).

## Why flagged

An incorrect deadline on file is worse than no deadline — it risks either a
missed submission window or wasted urgency around a date that doesn't exist.
Correcting it now, while there's still 50 days of runway, avoids both.

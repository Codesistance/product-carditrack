# UK National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare publishes recommendations

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Published 2026-09-10 on gov.uk (chaired by Profs. Denniston and Hughes, informed by
12,000+ respondents), this report recommends a future MHRA framework built on: staged
("learner-phase") authorisation for AI models, mandatory lifetime/continuous
real-world monitoring of every AI-enabled device, a searchable public incident
registry, and expanded MHRA enforcement powers. It is a recommendation, not policy —
government has promised only a "formal reply later," with no date given.

## Why this matters to CardiTrack

This is the clearest public signal yet of what MHRA's still-unpublished dedicated
AI-in-medical-devices framework (flagged as "expected later in 2026" since the
2026-09-04 digest — AI Airlock Phase 2) may contain once formalized. It does not move
today's SaMD boundary or change what CardiTrack can currently claim. But if adopted,
CardiTrack's MedGemma-narrated severity flags would likely face staged/monitored
authorisation and ongoing post-market surveillance obligations rather than a one-time
clearance, and safety-incident data could become part of a public registry. Watch item,
not an action item, until MHRA's formal response or a draft framework lands.

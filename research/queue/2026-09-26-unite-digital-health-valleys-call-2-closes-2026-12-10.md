# UNITE European Digital Health Valleys second open call — up to €1m per interregional project, closes 2026-12-10 17:00 CET; Scotland and Wales are eligible regions

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** grants
**Date recorded:** 2026-09-26

## Summary

UNITE (European Digital Health Valleys) is a €20m EU-co-funded project connecting regional
digital-health ecosystems. Its **second open call for joint interregional projects** opened on
2026-09-10 and closes **2026-12-10 at 17:00 CET**. Budget: €6m for up to six projects, maximum €1m
per project and €600k per beneficiary, 18 months, running 2027-04-01 to 2028-09-30. An online
information session is scheduled for 2026-09-30, 14:00–15:30 CEST.

**Eligibility as reported.** Each consortium must include at least three organisations from at least
three UNITE regions, covering a solution provider (company), a research entity and an end-user
organisation, with at least one partner from a "strong" region (Scotland, Northern Netherlands) and
at least one from a moderate/emerging region (Central Macedonia, Romania, Lombardy, Extremadura,
Aragón — the last added for call 2). Wales joined UNITE in July 2026 (the Welsh Government put over
£700k into participation) and Scottish Enterprise supports Scottish applicants. Themes cited by
Scottish Enterprise: health equity through deep-tech, and healthcare-workforce pressure through
digital solutions — a family-caregiver remote-monitoring product maps to the second. The first call's
winners (EISMEA, 2026-05-29) were remote-care and data-sharing projects.

**Fit.** Partial. CardiTrack qualifies only as, or alongside, a Scottish or Welsh entity, with two EU
partners from the required region mix. The 75-day runway is dominated by consortium-building, not
writing.

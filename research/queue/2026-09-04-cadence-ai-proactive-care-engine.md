# Cadence — AI Proactive Care Engine, national Medicare rollout

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** competition

## Summary

Cadence scaled its AI-powered Proactive Care Engine nationally in 2026 for Advanced
Primary Care Management, continuously monitoring chronic-disease patients (including
hypertension) and escalating anomalies to an NP-led clinical team embedded in Epic and
other major EMRs.

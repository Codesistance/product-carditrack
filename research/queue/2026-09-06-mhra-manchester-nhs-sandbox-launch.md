# MHRA and Manchester NHS launch a real-world testing sandbox for AI/wearable health tech

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

MHRA and Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust launched the "Manchester Sandbox"
(2026-09-02): a real-world testing programme letting innovators deploy AI-enabled
devices, wearables, and Ambient Voice Technology directly into NHS care pathways under
regulatory oversight. A stakeholder roundtable is planned later in 2026 to shape future
projects. Wearables and AVT products were demonstrated at the launch event.

## Why this matters for CardiTrack

A possible future evidence-generation/pilot route into the NHS for a caregiver-facing AI
cardiac-monitoring product, and a data point on MHRA's direction on real-world AI
oversight (consistent with the MHRA AI Airlock and Ambient Voice Technology guidance
already tracked). No immediate action — worth revisiting once the roundtable outcomes or
an application process are published.

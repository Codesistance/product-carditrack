# MHRA AI Airlock Phase 2 concludes; dedicated UK AI-as-medical-device framework due in 2026

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

MHRA's AI Airlock regulatory sandbox completed its second phase (7 innovators; focus on risk
classification, change-management planning, bias/fairness metrics for AI medical tools) in May
2026. MHRA has confirmed a dedicated AI-as-medical-device regulatory framework will be published in
2026 as part of the government's Life Sciences Sector Plan, informed by the National Commission
into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare, which published its findings 11 June 2026.

# FDA — Clinical Decision Support Software guidance, revised (2026-01-06 / re-issued 01-29)

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Finalized alongside the General Wellness guidance (see companion item). Refines the
Non-Device CDS carve-out under the 21st Century Cures Act, expanding enforcement
discretion for AI/generative-AI CDS that gives a single recommendation a clinician can
independently verify — but explicitly scoped to **HCP-facing CDS only**. No new policy
for consumer- or caregiver-facing tools, chatbots, or symptom checkers.

## Sources

- https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software

## Why flagged

Confirms the Non-Device CDS carve-out is **not** a path CardiTrack can rely on, since its
audience is family caregivers, not clinicians. This closes off one potential
"we're not a device because we're just CDS" argument before anyone spends time on it.

## Question to answer next

None required — this rules a path out rather than opening one. No action needed unless
CardiTrack ever adds an HCP-facing surface, at which point this guidance would become
directly relevant again.

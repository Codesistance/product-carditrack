# EU — MDCG Manual on Borderline and Classification, Version 5 (2026-04-22)

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Published 2026-04-22 by the Borderline and Classification Working Group (an MDCG
subgroup). Adds new digital-health/AI borderline examples and Helsinki Procedure rulings
on when software qualifies (or is excluded) as Medical Device Software (MDSW) under EU
MDR/IVDR — the most direct EU-level treatment of the wellness/medical-purpose line since
MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1 (June 2025, predates this run's window).

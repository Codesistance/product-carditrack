# MHRA and Manchester NHS launch a real-world AI-device evaluation sandbox

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

MHRA and Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust launched the "Manchester Sandbox" on
2026-09-02: a real-world NHS testbed, separate from the existing AI Airlock programme, for
evaluating emerging technologies — including AI-enabled medical devices, wearables, and Ambient
Voice Technology products — before wider adoption.

# MHRA — Ambient Voice Technology (AVT)-enabled products guidance (2026-07-29)

**Severity:** FYI
**Category:** regulation

## Summary

Published 2026-07-29 with NHS England, covering AI scribe/ambient-voice products — a
different product category from CardiTrack, but useful as a precedent for MHRA's current
reasoning pattern: AI output that is transcribed/summarized **for a clinician to review
before any action** stays outside medical device regulation; AI that supports diagnosis
or triggers **automated action** is regulated.

## Sources

- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ambient-voice-technology-enabled-products/ambient-voice-technology-enabled-products

## Why flagged

CardiTrack's push notifications trigger caregiver awareness/action based on an
**automated** severity verdict — no human clinician reviews the raw data first. Applying
MHRA's stated dividing line, that sits closer to the "automated action" side than the
AVT carve-out does. Not a direct rule on CardiTrack, but useful for internal risk
framing while the UK-specific AI/SaMD test (see companion AI Airlock item) is still being
written.

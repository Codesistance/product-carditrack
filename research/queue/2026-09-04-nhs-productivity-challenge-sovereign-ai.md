# NHS Productivity Challenge (Sovereign AI R&D Procurement Scheme) — deadline 2026-10-01

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

A newly launched (~Sept 2026) £100m UK government procurement scheme. The NHS-focused
stream funds R&D contracts (£250k-£10m, most £1-3m) for AI systems that automate
administrative workflows, coordinate care, or support clinical decision-making, in
support of the NHS 10-Year Health Plan. Run with DHSC via Innovate UK's Innovation
Funding Service; companies retain their IP. First of three rounds closes **2026-10-01**
(27 days from this run); further rounds close 2026-12-01 and 2027-02-01.

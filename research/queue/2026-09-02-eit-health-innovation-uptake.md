# EIT Health — Innovation Uptake Call 2026

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

Up to €650,000 per project (up to 8 projects funded), covering up to 50% of costs (co-funding
required), for consortia of a company plus a university/health-system partner to scale mature,
data-driven, AI-powered healthcare solutions across European markets.

## Source links

- https://www.eit.europa.eu/our-activities/opportunities/innovation-uptake-call
- https://eithealth.eu/opportunity/innovation-uptake/

## Why flagged

Deadline is **16 September 2026** — 14 days from this run, same window as the Transformative
Healthcare Instrument (item above). Relevant if CardiTrack has, or could quickly form, an NHS/EU
health-system partner interested in scaling remote cardiac monitoring adoption — the consortium
requirement is the key eligibility gate, not just company maturity.

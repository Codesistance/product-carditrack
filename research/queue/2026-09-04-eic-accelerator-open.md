**Severity:** FYI

## Summary

EU deep-tech scale-up instrument: grant up to ~€2.5m, plus optional equity for
EU-member applicants. UK applicants are eligible but restricted to the **grant-only**
track (no EIC Fund equity, since the UK isn't associated to the EIC Fund itself). Short
proposals accepted on a rolling basis, cut off the first Tuesday of each month
(next: 2026-10-06); full-proposal deadlines occur roughly every two months
(next: 2026-11-04).

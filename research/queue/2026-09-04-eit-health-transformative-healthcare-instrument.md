# EIT Health — Transformative Healthcare Instrument 2026 — deadline 2026-09-16

**Severity:** HIGH
**Category:** grants

## Summary

Single-beneficiary grant (no consortium required) of €300k-500k (up to 50% of costs) for
SMEs in digital health/AI/medtech with ≥4 FTEs that have raised ≥€2m in equity in the
last 36 months, to accelerate product development and commercialisation. UK entities are
eligible as a Horizon Europe associated country. Deadline **2026-09-16** — 12 days from
this run.
